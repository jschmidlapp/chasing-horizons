## Coit Lake

Very pretty off of Coit Road, near the center of the park. Nice picnic table and bathrooms on the southern side of the lake. The singletrack on the west side of the lake is a bit challening as much of it is pretty off camber.

## Crest Rd / Willow Ridge Rd

This is nominally a fire road but feels more like dirt double track in a lot of places. Surprisingly fun especially after a climb out of Kelly Lake.

![Crest Rd / Willow Ridge Rd](/media/trips/crest-rd-willow-ridge-rd/193e4e481a.jpg)
![Crest Rd / Willow Ridge Rd](/media/trips/crest-rd-willow-ridge-rd/b890ab85f8.jpg)

## Coit Road

Major fire road that goes from Hunting Hollow entrance and goes across most of the top of the southwestern part of the park. Still gets pretty steep at points, but mostly a reasonable grade by Henry Coe standards.
