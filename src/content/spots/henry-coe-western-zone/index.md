## Mile Trail

Hiking only trail that begins at the remnants of the Madrone Soda Springs resort and continues down to China Hole. Crosses back and forth across the creek a dozen times. Might be impassable if the water was high.

![Mile Trail](/media/trips/mile-trail/c8847694ff.jpg)

![Mile Trail](/media/trips/mile-trail/54ab5094c3.jpg)
![Mile Trail](/media/trips/mile-trail/1dfa40a207.jpg)

## Creekside Trail

Hiked up hill from China Hole. Technically bike legal but I don't know why you would ride it since you'd spend most of your time walking.

![Creekside Trail](/media/trips/creekside-trail/b0bf979827.jpg)

## Middle Ridge Trail

Long steep hike up from China Hole. Bike legal, but at least the lower part would be a very difficult/impossible climb and not much fun downhill, at least for me.

![Middle Ridge Trail](/media/trips/middle-ridge-trail/2674dc3508.jpg)
