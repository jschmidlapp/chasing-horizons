Steep trail which I rode from the top of Phegley Ridge down to Hunting Hollow Road. There aren't a lot of great places to brake so the ride was pretty harrowing at times. Still pretty fun, it's unfortunate that to get there you have to go up or down Wagon Road which isn't much fun. I did it on my traditional bike, so maybe tolerable on my eMTB.
