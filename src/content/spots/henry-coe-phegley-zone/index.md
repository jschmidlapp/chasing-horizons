## Fish Pond

Nice little pond a bit off of Hunting Hollow Road. The singletrack here is barely marked and pretty easy to miss.

![Fish Pond](/media/trips/fish-pond/f03ade4ca9.jpg)

## Vasquez Road

Steep and boring fire road. I just rode this a bit to explore another trail.

![Vasquez Road](/media/trips/vasquez-road/788334bd18.jpg)
![Vasquez Road](/media/trips/vasquez-road/723eb37bed.jpg)

## Long Dam Trail

Fun trail but out of the way and difficult to get to, which was evident by the fact that the trail just plain disappears sometimes.

![Long Dam Trail](/media/trips/long-dam-trail/b2bb4edfca.jpg)
![Long Dam Trail](/media/trips/long-dam-trail/ce549c831f.jpg)

## Wagon Road

Steep and loose fire road. This really isn't any fun in either direction. Possibly tolerable up on my eMTB. Maybe.

![Wagon Road](/media/trips/wagon-road/73131e3aea.jpg)

## Redfern Trail

Steep trail which I rode from the top of Phegley Ridge down to Hunting Hollow Road. There aren't a lot of great places to brake so the ride was pretty harrowing at times. Still pretty fun, it's unfortunate that to get there you have to go up or down Wagon Road which isn't much fun. I did it on my traditional bike, so maybe tolerable on my eMTB.

![Redfern Trail](/media/trips/redfern-trail/6ecd8b8491.jpg)
