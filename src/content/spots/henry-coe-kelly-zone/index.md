## Kelly Lake

Nice lake, with a catch. It's accessible via both Coit Road and Kelly Lake Trail. Coit Road is fine, but Kelly Lake Trail is crazy steep in either direction. Riding up from the lake west to east looks just impossible to me, and coming down just seems terrifying. I'm sure much of that is a skill issue, but still. There is Kelly Creek Trail that also goes out, which is fine in summer but I suspect is impassable in the winter.
