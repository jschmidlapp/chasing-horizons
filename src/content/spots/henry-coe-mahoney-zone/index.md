## Coit Spring Trail

Top part is narrow singletrack but the bottom part after Cross Canyon is wide doubletrack (looks like an overgrown ranch road). Fine for what it is but unless you need to get back to Coit Rd faster / closer to the ridge I would take Grapevine.

![Coit Spring Trail](/media/trips/coit-spring-trail/9f0f7353d1.jpg)
